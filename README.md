# CrispStat

**Keep your WhatsApp Status sharp.** CrispStat prepares your photos and videos before you post them, so WhatsApp has less reason to compress them into a blurry mess.

## Download

👉 **[Download the latest CrispStat.apk](https://github.com/Iamhalf/CrispStat/releases/latest)**

Open the link on your Android phone, tap **CrispStat.apk**, then tap the downloaded file to install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details → Install anyway** (normal for apps not from the Play Store).

## Features

- **720p or 1080p video**: resizes and re-encodes your video at a clean, steady quality that suits WhatsApp Status.
- **Auto-split long videos**: cuts videos into 30-second or 60-second parts, in order, ready to post one after another.
- **Photo resize**: shrinks very large photos to a sensible size with high quality.
- **Share to WhatsApp**: one tap opens WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business); choose **My status**.
- **Save to Gallery**: saves results to `Movies/CrispStat` or `Pictures/CrispStat`.
- **Share into the app**: share a video from your Gallery straight into CrispStat.
- No account, no ads, no internet needed. Everything happens on your phone.

## How to use

1. Choose **30 seconds** or **60 seconds** for the part length.
2. Choose **720p** or **1080p**.
3. Tap **Choose photo or video**.
4. Wait until it says **ready**.
5. Tap **Share to WhatsApp** → **My status**, or **Save to Gallery**.

## Tips

- If 1080p still looks soft after posting, try 720p. WhatsApp sometimes compresses large files again.
- In WhatsApp, turn on the **HD** option when it appears.
- Sending to a person in a chat? Send the video as a **Document** (📎 → Document) for full quality.
- CrispStat can't make a blurry original sharper. It protects the quality you already have.
